# Governance

FIOF is an independently usable FRITZ module. Interoperability and safety take precedence over integration convenience.

Maintainers review changes, record design decisions and nominate release owners. Contributors propose work through issues and pull requests. Breaking or safety-sensitive changes require written design review and a second reviewer. Record objections, disposition and rationale. Maintainers resolve disputes transparently while protecting private reports.

No named maintainer roster, contact address or hosted reporting channel was supplied. Establish them before public release under the [release checklist](docs/releases.md). No person is assumed to have accepted a support role.
