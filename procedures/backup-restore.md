# Backups and restore

Document protected data, repository locations, ownership, encryption, schedule, retention, failure alerts, off-site copies, and recovery objectives.

A successful backup job is not proof of recoverability. Validate repository health and perform scheduled restore tests of representative files, databases, mailboxes, applications, and full-system procedures.

Never delete or prune backups without explicit approval, exact target verification, retention review, and recoverability confirmation. Local backups on the same physical failure domain are not sufficient on their own.

Modified and generalized for FIOF 0.1.0. Apply the [core contract](../spec/core.md) and [knowledge contract](../spec/knowledge.md). Record prerequisites, authority, evidence, outcomes and next action. This procedure grants no additional authority.
