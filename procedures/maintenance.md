# Maintenance

Routine maintenance must have a defined scope, maintenance window, pre-check, backup requirement, rollback plan, and validation checklist.

Review updates before installation. Separate security patches, routine package updates, panel updates, and major upgrades. Never combine an unrelated upgrade with a repair.

Check capacity trends, failed services, backup results, certificate expiry, queues, filesystem health, log anomalies, and pending reboots. Record deferrals and their risk.

Modified and generalized for FIOF 0.1.0. Apply the [core contract](../spec/core.md) and [knowledge contract](../spec/knowledge.md). Record prerequisites, authority, evidence, outcomes and next action. This procedure grants no additional authority.
