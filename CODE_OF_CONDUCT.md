# Code of conduct

Be respectful, constructive and considerate of different backgrounds and experience. Critique work and reasoning; protect privacy. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing and deliberate disclosure of private information are unacceptable.

Maintainers may remove contributions or restrict participation proportionately, recording reasons privately when disclosure could harm affected people. Reports go to a designated private conduct contact, which must be appointed and published before public release. Do not post sensitive reports publicly. An implicated maintainer should recuse themselves; another designated maintainer should review appeals.
